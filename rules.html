Trading Rules

Simple rules, checked automatically

Every account runs against the same rule engine. You always know your limits and how much room is left.

Profit target

The profit you must reach to pass. Once reached within the rules, the account moves to funded.

Daily loss limit

The maximum you can lose in a single day. Crossing it ends the evaluation.

Maximum drawdown

The total your balance may fall from its peak. Stay above it and the account stays active.

Minimum trading days

The least number of days you must trade, so results come from a real process.

Consistency rule

Your biggest day cannot make up too large a share of total profit.

Contract limits

A cap on contracts per account size, keeping position sizing in line with capital.

Rules are applied from the first trade. Any change to a program is announced before it applies to new accounts.

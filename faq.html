FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers about evaluations, funded accounts and payouts.

What is VexoTrade?

VexoTrade is a proprietary trading firm. Traders pass an evaluation, then trade a funded account and keep most of the profits.

Do I trade real money?

Evaluation and funded accounts run in a simulated environment. Payouts are based on simulated performance under program rules.

What instruments can I trade?

We focus on CME futures. Other markets may be added later.

How do I get paid?

Once you meet the payout conditions, request a payout from your dashboard. Each request is reviewed before approval.

What happens if I break a rule?

Breaking a daily loss limit or maximum drawdown marks the account as failed. The rules engine applies these checks automatically.

Can I reset a failed account?

Reset options will be published with final program pricing.

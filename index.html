Trading Rules

Simple rules, checked automatically

Every account runs against the same rule engine. You always know your limits and how much room is left.

Profit Target

The profit you must reach to pass. Once hit — within the rules — your account moves to funded.

Daily Loss Limit

The maximum you can lose in a single day. Cross it and the evaluation ends.

Maximum Drawdown

The total your balance may fall from its peak. Stay above it and your account stays alive.

Minimum Trading Days

The least number of days you must trade — so results come from a real process.

Consistency Rule

Your biggest day can't make up too large a share of total profit.

Contract Limits

A cap on contracts per account size, keeping position sizing in line with your capital.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is a prop firm, exactly?
A proprietary trading firm funds traders with its own capital. You prove your skill in an evaluation, then trade a funded account and share the profits with the firm.
What do I need to pass the evaluation?
Reach the profit target for your account size while staying within the daily loss limit and maximum drawdown, trade the minimum number of days, and respect the consistency rule. All limits are shown before you start.
How much of the profit do I keep?
Funded traders keep up to 90% of the profits generated on their account. Your share and payout history are always visible in your dashboard.
Which markets and platforms can I trade?
VexoTrade is built for CME-listed futures (ES, NQ, CL, GC and more), and connects with tools like NinjaTrader, Tradovate, TradingView, Rithmic and Quantower.
How fast are payouts?
Once your funded account meets the conditions, you can request a payout on a short cycle and track it through every status — requested, under review, approved and paid.
When will pricing be available?
Final challenge pricing and the exact rule values are being confirmed and will be published shortly. The figures on this page are indicative examples.

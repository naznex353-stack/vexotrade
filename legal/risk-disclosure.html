Trading futures carries substantial risk

Futures contracts are leveraged instruments. Small price movements can produce large gains or losses, and you can lose more than you expect. Only trade with capital you can afford to lose.

Simulated accounts

VexoTrade evaluation and funded accounts are simulated. Results in a simulated account may differ from live trading because of execution, liquidity, slippage and psychological factors. Simulated results are not a guarantee of future performance.

Rules are strict

A breach of a daily loss limit, maximum drawdown or other program rule ends the account and may prevent any payout. Plan your position size and stop-losses with the rules in mind.

No investment advice

VexoTrade does not recommend any trade, instrument or strategy. You are responsible for your own trading decisions.

Technology risk

Internet outages, platform failures and third-party outages can prevent you from managing positions. Keep your own risk controls in place.
